Privacy policy

Mosskite Games is operated by Mosskite LLC. This policy describes this website’s current practices. Our games are still in development.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Prelaunch notice. No Mosskite game has been announced or released. Game data practices, providers, and retention periods have not been finalized. We will update this policy with accurate game details before a game is submitted to an app store.

This website

This is a static information site. It has no account, contact form, analytics, advertising scripts, or cookies set by Mosskite LLC. When you visit, our site delivery providers may receive technical request information such as your IP address, browser details, and the pages requested to deliver and secure the site. We do not receive a visitor list from this site.

Games and services

Our games are in development. We have not yet selected any advertising SDK, analytics SDK, purchase provider, or leaderboard service for a released game. We will identify any services we use, the data they collect or receive, their purposes, and links to their privacy information here before release. We will also describe any in-app purchases and how they are processed.

A nickname-only leaderboard is being considered. If implemented, we will explain what nickname and score data is collected, whether device or account identifiers are involved, who receives the data, and how scores can be removed. No player accounts are planned at this time.

Retention and security

There is no player data stored by this website. Before a game launches, we will state its data retention periods and the measures used to protect player data, based on its actual implementation and providers.

Requests and changes

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email support@mosskite.net. You can also visit our support page. If a leaderboard launches, this address will accept score removal requests. If future games add player accounts, we will provide both an in-app deletion option and an external request path before release.

We will update this page when our games or data practices change and revise the date above.
