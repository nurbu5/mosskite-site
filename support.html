Support

Need help with a Mosskite game? We want to hear from you.

Email us at support@mosskite.net. We can help with game questions and privacy requests.

Game questions

In your email, include the game name, your device and operating system, and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

Score removal

If a future game has a nickname leaderboard, you will be able to request removal of your score by emailing support@mosskite.net. Include the game name, displayed nickname, and enough information to identify the score. We may ask for details to verify the request.
